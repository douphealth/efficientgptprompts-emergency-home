How to use this resource

Start with the outcome you need, not the tool you want to use. A strong AI workflow states the business context, the target reader, the decision being supported, the constraints the model must respect, and the format the final answer must follow.

  • For SEO: define search intent, section coverage, snippet targets, trust links, and internal-link destinations.
  • For AEO: write direct answers under question headings before adding detail.
  • For GEO: make paragraphs self-contained, define entities, and cite or explain the reasoning behind claims.
  • For revenue: connect the prompt output to a CTA, offer, audit, product, or next action.
